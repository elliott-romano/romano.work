Instrument MFA Thesis

2025 Print, Research

Instrument is the culmination of a three-year inquiry into what it means to design tools that perform. It is part research project and part artist book. The thesis explores how real-time feedback systems, both analog and digital, can function as graphic instruments: systems that generate visual and linguistic output through performance.

Introduction

Through essays, interviews, and custom tools of my own design, the book frames design as an instrumental practice that exists in the space between recording and live expression.

Process

The development of Instrument involved extensive research into the history of graphic design tools, interviews with practitioners working at the intersection of design and performance, and the creation of custom software tools that demonstrate the principles explored in the thesis.

Media

Instrument book cover and interior spreads

Interactive demonstration of the thesis concepts

Conclusion

Instrument proposes a new framework for understanding design as a performative practice, where tools become instruments for creative expression rather than static objects for production.
