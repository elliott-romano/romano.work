Docker Tag Mutability/Gordon

I consulted with Docker Inc, to design and introduce new features for Dockerhub, a modern container registry system. The functionality I helped ship allows developers to programmatically manage their repositories, and interact with Gordon, Docker's primary AI agent to assist with new learning about new processes or automating mundane work, all of which make primary workflows feel lighter and easier.

DigitalOcean Product Onboarding & Growth

As a design lead on DigitalOceans Growth team, I led user research studies and design Initiatives that made using cloud infrastructure feel light and understandable for a previously underserved market - developers and makers with limited cloud experience. My work included the release of many measurable design and interface experiments that informed several iterative system-wide interface updates to the first time user experience of the DigitalOcean Platform.

RISD Museum Digital Guide

I had the opportunity to collaborate with museum curatorial and digital initiatives staff to conceptualize and design the primary digital visitor application for the RISD Museum which aimed to help visitors discover and navigate the challenging architectural footprint of the gallery spaces, as well as its vast collection of works and associated media.

Instrument MFA Thesis (2025)

Instrument is a thesis project exploring the intersection of typography, technology, and human interaction. Through custom software and experimental printing techniques, this work investigates how digital tools can enhance rather than replace traditional design processes, creating new possibilities for visual communication.

CTC (2024)

CTC is a collaborative project exploring computational design methodologies in contemporary practice. This work combines algorithmic thinking with traditional design principles to create dynamic visual systems that respond to data and user interaction.

Various Flora (2025)

Various Flora was a public exhibition at the RISD Museum in Providence, RI. The exhibition featured a collection of Machine-Learning generated flora trained from the museum's vast collection of botanical prints. Each piece explored the intersection of traditional botanical illustration and contemporary AI-generated imagery, creating hybrid forms that question the boundaries between natural and artificial representation.

Cellular Autonomies (2024)

Cellular Autonomies is a study into natural systems as a means of generating improvised procedural form. Using custom software based on Stephen Wolfram's original Cellular Autonoma algorithms as a means of generative pattern making, a series of monoprints further abstract the gesture of unpredicatble growth from one element to the next. The collection exists as a series of large scale monoprints and limited edition risograph booklets.

LRAD (2024)

LRAD was a research project into the use of sound and control, diving into the history and usage of Long-Range-Acoustic-Device (LRAD). The resulting print and digital publications and explores the visual and material language of "tactical sound" as the LRAD becomes a more ubiquitous in its use in policing and protest.

Chladni Sans (2024)

Chladni Sans is an experimental typeface that explores the intersection of sound, vibration, and visual form. Inspired by Chladni figures—patterns created by sound waves on vibrating plates—this typeface translates acoustic phenomena into letterforms, creating a unique visual language that bridges the gap between auditory and visual perception.
