Instrument MFA Thesis

Print Design Research

Instrument is the culmination of a three-year inquiry into what it means to design tools that perform. It is part research project and part artist book. The thesis explores how real-time feedback systems, both analog and digital, can function as graphic instruments: systems that generate visual and linguistic output through performance. Through essays, interviews, and custom tools of my own design, the book frames design as an instrumental practice that exists in the space between recording and live expression.

Docker Tag Mutability

Product Design UX

I designed and shipped Tag Mutability for Docker Hub, enabling teams to safely and flexibly manage how image tags are updated. This gave organizations stronger governance over critical workflows—reducing accidental overwrites, improving auditability, and aligning Docker Hub with enterprise CI/CD needs. I defined the user experience across multiple surfaces in and outside of the UI and CLI. The work extended existing platform patterns and shipped features like per-repository settings, tag retention rules, and audit-friendly activity visibility.
