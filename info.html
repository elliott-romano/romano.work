Profile

Elliott [b. 1989] is a graphic designer/educator specializing in product, motion, & interactive design projects.

His studio and research practice investigates the design of software and expressive tools that lead to meaningful non-deterministic outcomes.

He holds an masters degree from Rhode Island School and based in the pacific northwest.
